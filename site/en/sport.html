Challenge and solution

Every announcement heard in every stand

In gyms, arenas and stadiums, sound bounces off concrete, glass and ice or escapes into open space. The result is familiar: the announcer is unintelligible in the far stands, the front rows are deafened, and outdoors the neighbors hear everything. Pools and ice rinks add humidity to the mix.

We design the system around the venue’s geometry and aim the sound at the audience, not at the walls, the roof or beyond the stadium. We handle installation and tuning ourselves.

  • See the result before you buy: we model the room in EASE and show you the SPL map before delivery.
  • Even coverage of the stands: TAURA and ERA arrays, compact CX for training halls.
  • For humid spaces and outdoor venues, IP55-rated equipment.
