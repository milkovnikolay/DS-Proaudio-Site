What you get

Not a catalog, but a solution for your venue

We design and manufacture our loudspeakers ourselves, so we know how each model will behave in a specific room.

An engineer will choose the series and quantity, taking into account the hall’s purpose, capacity, budget and installation requirements.

How the consultation works

Five steps from task to project

  1. 01

    Tell us about your venue

    Type of hall, capacity, task: speech, music, concerts. You can do it by phone.

  2. 02

    Send us the details

    A floor plan or room dimensions, photos, and a design or specification if you have one.

  3. 03

    Options

    The engineer offers one or two solutions and explains the difference.

  4. 04

    Simulation and specification

    Acoustic model, equipment list and documents.

  5. 05

    Listening session

    You listen to the selected system at our showroom or at your venue.

What to prepare for a quick consultation

  • Plan and dimensionsA drawing, an evacuation plan or simply the hall’s length, width and height.
  • Purpose and capacityWhat will be played: speech, background music, concerts. How many people in the audience.
  • Timeline and purchasingWhen you need the system and how the purchase will be handled.

FAQ

What to know in advance

Is the consultation paid?
The initial consultation and system specification using DS PROAUDIO equipment are free. The scope of simulation for large projects is discussed separately.
Do I need a finished design?
No. The room dimensions and an understanding of the task are enough. If you already have a design, we’ll review it and suggest a system to match.
.
Where can I get GLL files and drawings?
All files for each model are in the Documentation Center: EASE GLL, DWG, manuals and DSP presets.
Who will install the system?
Our integrator partners in your region or your own contractor. We’ll help at every stage. Learn more on the page Where to buy.
