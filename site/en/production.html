About us · Full-cycle manufacturing

We don’t assemble loudspeakers from other people’s parts. We manufacture them.

Enclosures, metalwork, voice coils, surrounds, LF drivers, HF compression drivers, waveguides and electronics are all made in our own workshops in St. Petersburg and the Leningrad Region.
