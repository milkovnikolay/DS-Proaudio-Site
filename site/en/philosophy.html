About us

Philosophy

Our product philosophy

Scroll down
01
Respect for our peers

We have deep respect for our peers at other manufacturers, especially those who change the industry and set its quality standards. We study their experience closely and admire their breakthroughs.

02
Our own path

But we follow our own path. We never copy other people’s solutions: even when a technology catches our interest, we rebuild it from scratch until we arrive at a better result.

03
Team

We are a large team of like-minded people, and each of us puts our best into the common cause.

04
Care for everyone

And we care about everyone who touches our equipment at every stage of its life cycle: not only the listener, but also the sound engineers, system engineers and installation partners.

Principles that took shape while developing ERA

  1. Deliver undistorted music to as many listeners in the room as possible.
  2. Put consistent tonal balance ahead of equal loudness.
  3. Reproduce sound the way the musicians and engineers intended it, rather than “improving” it with a sound of our own.
In practiceA telling example of how this works in practice is how we developed ERA (see History): instead of the traditional asymmetrical design of scalable point sources, the team moved to a coaxial waveguide arrangement, to make coverage of the venue more even and deliver undistorted sound to more listeners. This decision gave rise to the principle of not coloring the sound with a signature of our own, but staying disciplined and keeping out of its way.
