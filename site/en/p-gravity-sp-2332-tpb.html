Description

Gravity SP 2332 TPB: sub-to-satellite pole for loudspeakers, 35 mm tube diameter, M20 thread.

The stand comes in two parts, joined with an M20 rubber coupling.

Material: steel

Color: black

Minimum working length: 1050 mm

Maximum working length: 1400 mm

Collapsed length: 540 mm

Maximum load: 40 kg

Weight: 2.2 kg
