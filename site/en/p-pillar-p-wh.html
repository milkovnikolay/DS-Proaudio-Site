Description

Assembled, the system stands 237 cm tall, so it can be deployed without extra risers or lifts if needed.

Even coverage, high speech intelligibility and wide dispersion (over 140°), together with the design, make it an excellent choice for conferences. Subwoofers with a solid frequency range and high SPL let it handle far more demanding tasks.

Passive column loudspeaker (6×5” drivers + 2×1.5” HF drivers on a waveguide), frequency range: 140–20,000 Hz, max SPL: 137 dB, horizontal dispersion: 140°, vertical dispersion: +9°/-21°, RMS power: 800 W, impedance: 5.4 Ω, dimensions: 1300×150×210, weight: 23 kg.

Passive subwoofer: 2×12” LF drivers in a bass-reflex enclosure, frequency range: 40–300 Hz (±3 dB), max SPL: 137 dB, power: 1200 W (4800 W peak), impedance: 4 Ω, dimensions: 1070×330×400 mm, weight 40 kg; input/output: 2× Speakon NL4.

When we supply a complete system with amplification and processing, we load the factory presets into the processor. Alternatively, we provide the data so you can enter the corrections into your own processor.

Technologies

Patent No. 2786503

Horizontal waveguide summing

Multiple drivers combine into a single coherent wavefront: in-phase summation across the full frequency range and symmetrical horizontal dispersion with no interference nulls.

Used in: TAURA 8, PLR TOP III (PILLAR), STL TOP III (STELLA)

Proprietary technology

Edge-wound interlocked voice coils

Flat wire wound on edge, locked in place by our proprietary interlocking method: the voice coil withstands up to 400 °C, and the driver holds its parameters at full rated power.

Used in: all DS PROAUDIO series
